Twelve Yards

Five kicks each from the spot. Level after five, and it goes to sudden death.

Your kick
Flick up from the ball toward your corner. A faster flick is a harder shot; bend the flick to curl it.
Their kick
Move the gloves across the goal and get them behind the ball.
This game needs WebGL, which this browser or device has switched off.
